# Growth insights guide

Growth insights shows when to post, what to post, how you compare with others, and where people drop out of your DM funnel. It only reads your data — it never sends messages and never changes your Instagram/Facebook automations.

## Anomaly alerts

**How it works:** Every few minutes the page compares the last 24 hours with your usual day (average of the 7 days before). It warns you when comments/DMs received or automated DMs sent fall to half or less, or when failed sends double. If you have fewer than 5 per day on average, drops are not flagged (too little data).

**How to use:**
1. Open **Growth insights** — alerts show at the top.
2. "All normal" means nothing unusual. A red alert means a big change — check **Trust & safety** and **Social accounts** first.

## Best time to post

**How it works:** We count every comment and DM your audience sent in the last 60 days, by day and hour (India time). The busiest 3 slots are your best times. At least 10 comments/DMs are needed before suggestions appear.

**How to use:**
1. Open the **Best time to post** tab.
2. Note the top slots (for example "Sun 7 PM").
3. Schedule your next posts in **Create post** about 30 minutes before that slot.

## Funnel heatmap

**How it works:** For the last 30 days we count each step: Comment/DM → DM sent → Delivery page opened → Link clicked → Sale (Razorpay paid). Each row shows the count per weekday; darker boxes mean more activity. The % under each step shows how many moved on from the previous step.

**How to use:**
1. Open **Funnel heatmap**.
2. Find the step with the lowest %. Example: DM sent 100, Page opened 20 → improve the DM text or button.
3. Change that step and compare again after a week.

## AI content ideas

**How it works:** AI reads your niche, goal and recent captions, and suggests 6 ideas with a hook, a format (Reel/Carousel/Story/Post) and a comment keyword you can use in an automation. Each click uses 1 of your daily AI requests.

**How to use:**
1. Type your niche, e.g. "home baking classes", and optional goal, e.g. "sell my ebook".
2. Press **Generate ideas**.
3. Pick an idea, post it, and create an automation with the suggested keyword (e.g. comment "RECIPE" → DM the link).

## Competitor benchmark

**How it works:** Meta does not share other accounts' private stats, so you type competitor numbers from their public profile (followers, posts per week, average comments). Your own posts/week and comments per post come from the last 28 days. Engagement = average comments per post ÷ followers × 100.

**How to use:**
1. Enter your follower count at the top.
2. Owners, admins and managers can add competitors in **Add a competitor**.
3. Read the tips under the table — e.g. "Competitors post ~5 times a week; you post 1".
