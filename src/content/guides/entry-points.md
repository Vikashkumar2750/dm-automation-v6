# Ice breakers & menu

## How it works

- **Ice breakers** are up to 4 ready questions people see when they open your DMs for the **first time** (no earlier chat with you). Tapping one sends that question to you as a normal DM.
- **Menu** (Facebook Messenger only) is a list of up to 5 buttons always available in the chat. A button with a link opens the link; a button without a link sends its title as a DM.
- Nothing appears on Instagram or Facebook until you press **Sync to Meta**. Saving alone only stores it here.
- Your automations answer these taps: a tap arrives like any DM, so a **New DM** rule (or flow) with the question as its keyword replies automatically.

## How to use

1. Go to **Social accounts** and make sure Instagram / Facebook show **Connected**.
2. In **Ice breakers & menu**, type your questions (or pick a ready-made set). Add menu buttons if you use Facebook.
3. Press **Save**, then **Sync to Meta**. Check the result box: every account should show ✓ live on Meta.
4. Go to **Automations** → create a **New DM** rule for each question, match type **Exact** or **Contains**, keyword = the question text, and write the reply.
5. Turn the rule on.

## Testing checklist

- Use a **different** Instagram/Facebook account that has **never** messaged you — ice breakers only show in a brand-new chat.
- On Instagram, open your profile → **Message**. The questions appear above the keyboard. Tap one; your rule should reply within seconds.
- Changes on Meta can take a few minutes to show. Close and reopen the app.
- If sync shows ✗ "reconnect this account first", reconnect that account in Social accounts, then sync again.
- Clearing all questions and syncing removes them from Meta.
